BEWARE: OTA Hidden Charges & Commission Scams

Many online travel agencies (OTAs) and metasearch platforms add hidden fees that inflate the ticket price:

  • Convenience Fee: Rs.200-600 per passenger added at checkout (MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Cleartrip, Yatra add this)
  • Insurance/Add-ons: Pre-selected travel insurance, seat selection, meals auto-added to cart - UNCHECK these
  • Payment Gateway Fee: Extra 1-2% charged on credit/debit card payments by some OTAs
  • Dynamic Pricing Markup: OTAs may show a low base fare but add markup in taxes/fees breakdown
  • Commission Baked In: OTAs get 1-5% commission from airlines, sometimes they add more on top

SOLUTION: Always compare the FINAL price (after all fees) across platforms. Book directly on airline websites when possible - they have ZERO convenience fee and no middleman markup. Use EaseMyTrip if booking via OTA (zero convenience fee).

Pro Tips for Brutally Cheap Flights

Follow these to consistently find the lowest fares.

🔎

Use Incognito/Private Mode

Airlines and OTAs track your searches and may raise prices. Always search in incognito/private browsing mode. This app stores nothing, but the booking sites do.

📅

Be Flexible with Dates

Tuesday, Wednesday, and Saturday departures are usually cheapest. Use Google Flights' date grid or Skyscanner's "Whole Month" view to find the cheapest day.

⏰

Book 3-8 Weeks in Advance

For domestic flights, 3-6 weeks ahead is the sweet spot. For international, 6-12 weeks. Last-minute bookings are almost always more expensive.

🌏

Consider Nearby Airports

Flying into a nearby city can save thousands. Example: BLR vs HYD for South India, or DEL vs JAI. Check both and compare.

💰

Stack Offers

Combine bank card offers + coupon codes + wallet cashback for maximum savings. Some combos can save Rs.3000-5000 on international flights.

✈

Check Direct Airline Sites

Sometimes airlines (especially IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet) have web-exclusive fares not shown on OTAs. Always cross-check the airline's own website.

🚨

Set Fare Alerts

Use Google Flights "Track prices" and Skyscanner "Price Alerts". Get notified when prices drop for your route. Patience often pays off.

🎫

Points > Cash on Premium Cabins

Miles/points give much better value when redeemed for business/first class (2x-5x better per-point value vs economy). If you have enough points, go premium.
